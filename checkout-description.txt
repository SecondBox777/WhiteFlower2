Mindscope is a self-service web application that sells personalized AI-generated reports, including a preliminary IQ estimate calculated by a predefined synthetic scoring model, for adult users exploring their problem-solving patterns. Customers learn about the product through our website, where they can complete a free 30-question test before purchasing a report. We plan to charge a one-time fee per report through Lemon Squeezy, with no subscriptions or recurring charges.
